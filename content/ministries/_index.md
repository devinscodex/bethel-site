There is a place for every age at Bethel. Children in grades K-6 meet on Monday afternoons for Kids' Klub and Children's Choir, teens gather on Wednesdays, and Sunday School has classes for all ages, including four for adults. Our Worship Team leads us in old and new songs, Women in Mission meet to quilt and share a Bible lesson, and Providence Fellowship Center, our building, is open to the community.
