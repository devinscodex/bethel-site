Our Bethel Youth Group includes teens in 7th – 12th grades. They meet on Wednesdays for worship, teaching, and snacks.  Most often they meet at Providence Fellowship Center but be sure to check your weekly bulletin for location.

Our youth try to do a service project every month. Typically this means going to the home of one of our older members and helping with yard work, cleaning, painting, etc.

Our teens have a heart for service as well as a heart for missions. They have done several mission projects and have plans to expand their reach soon!

For more information about the Bethel Youth Group, please see our [contact details](/about/#contact).
