The purpose of our Worship Team is to lead the Bethel family in worship as we approach the throne of God. We want everyone to feel welcome to worship in a way they feel comfortable. Some sit, some stand, and some raise their hands. We sing both old and new songs. Bethel Fellowship is a place that you can express yourself freely in worship.

Our Worship Team is open to anyone who has a heart for worship and can sing, play an instrument, or run our sound board. If you are interested in joining the team, please use the Contact Us tab.
