title: "Women in Mission"
blurb: "Quilting, fellowship and a Bible lesson"
---

Women in Mission meets at 1:00pm on the 3rd Thursday of each month for quilting, fellowship, and a Bible lesson. They also conduct BINGO at Good Shepherd Care Center in Versailles several times throughout the year.

Our Women in Mission hold a Comforter Day that meets on the last Wednesday of each month at 9:30am in the Fellowship Hall. They tack comforters that are eventually distributed to homeless citizens throughout the country.
