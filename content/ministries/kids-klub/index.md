blurb: "After school, grades K-6"

We have two after-school programs for children in grades K-6.
Children’s Choir is used to help children memorize the books of the Bible and develop a love for worship.

At Kids’ Klub, children are given a thorough overview of the Bible, encouraged to memorize Scripture, and challenged to be able to quickly find passages in the Bible.

Children’s Choir and Kids’ Klub meet weekly on Monday’s from 3:30-5:30 during the school year.
