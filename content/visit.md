We'd love to have you with us! Come as you are.

## Worship

Come in any of our four doors. Someone will greet you and hand you a bulletin. Sit anywhere, on the main floor or in the balcony, which has two nursing areas. If you would like us to reach out, fill out a Welcome Card from the pew.

## Sunday School

Classes for all ages at 9:30, including four for adults. Questions? Email [bethel.fec@gmail.com](mailto:bethel.fec@gmail.com).

## Children

A nursery for ages 4 and under during the sermon, downstairs, with three staff. Their names are in the bulletin.

## What to wear

Come as you are. Most people dress casually, and some dress up. Wear what is comfortable.

## Music

We sing old and new songs, led by our Worship Team. The children sing at least one Sunday a month.

## Prayer

Requests are printed on the back of the bulletin. To join the prayer list, ask the office, a deacon or the pastor.
