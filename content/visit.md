We'd love to have you with us! Come as you are.

## Sunday School

Classes for all ages at 9:30, including four for adults. Questions? Email [bethel.fec@gmail.com](mailto:bethel.fec@gmail.com).

## Worship

Use any of our four doors. Someone will greet you and hand you a bulletin. Sit on the floor or in the balcony, which has two nursing areas. Fill out a Welcome Card from the pew if you'd like us to reach out.

## What to wear

Most dress casually. Dress up if you like. Wear what is comfortable.

## Children

A nursery for ages 4 and under during the sermon, downstairs, with three staff. Their names are in the bulletin.

## Music

A blended mix of old and new songs, led by our Worship Team. The children sing at least one Sunday a month.

## Sermon

25 to 35 minutes of Bible teaching for everyday life. Bring a Bible or use your phone; we have WiFi. Scripture is on the screens. Sermons are posted the next Monday or Tuesday.

## Offering

2 Corinthians 9:7: "So let each one give as he purposes in his heart, not grudgingly or of necessity; for God loves a cheerful giver."

## Prayer

Requests are printed on the back of the bulletin. To join the prayer list, ask the office, a deacon or the pastor.

## Communion

The first Sunday of January, April, July and October. Open to everyone who has accepted the gift of salvation through Jesus Christ, member or not. Parents decide for their children.

## After church

Most people stay and visit. We hope you will too.

## Midweek

Children's Choir and Kids' Klub: Mondays, 3:30 to 5:30, during the school year. Youth: Wednesdays at 6:30 PM.
